Modifies the protection enchantment's damage reduction formula to be a configurable value.

Ideal for modpacks where enchants can go past the vanilla/default maximum, there's armor sets with higher amounts of armor and armor toughness than netherite, or any other case where using protection becomes cancerous for PvP and/or makes PvE trivial, while keeping protection is preferable.
